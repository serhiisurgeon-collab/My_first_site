My journey

From medicine
to digital solutions

Medical education, military service and teaching have shaped my professional focus. Practical work helps me identify topics that need clearer explanations and tools that could be useful.

I graduated from medical university in 2019 and completed an internship in forensic medicine in 2019–2021. I have served as a military physician since 2021. In 2025, I specialized in surgery.

In organizing medical support, I work to align personnel training, available resources, coordination and evacuation capabilities. Part of this planning is understanding what care receiving facilities can actually provide. I speak with medical leaders and treating clinicians to clarify specialist and diagnostic availability, blood supplies, and the capabilities and limitations of surgical care.

I teach TCCC/CLS to military personnel without a medical background. Participants bring different levels of experience, so I focus on what each person can understand and apply after training. My aim is to help them recognize a life-threatening condition, set priorities, provide care within the scope of their training, and understand when a medic’s involvement is needed.

I write professional articles, create educational materials and develop digital tools. These include S-Dose, an app I am developing for medication reference information and dose calculations. Across these projects, I value reliable sources, clear explanations and explicit limitations. Questions from practice and teaching help guide what I work on next.
