Questions about learning with the bot

Which languages can I learn?

Ukrainian, English, German and Spanish. Spanish has the largest collection; the other languages have A1/A2/B1 starter sets. These are practice materials, not a promise of a complete course.

Can I choose the menu and translation languages separately?

Yes. Set your interface, learning and translation languages independently. The translation language must differ from the learning language; the language of this website does not determine your bot settings.

What do the 12 learning directions mean?

Four learning languages × three other translation languages. For example, learn German with English translations. Choose your menu language separately.

How do I start and change my learning direction?

Open the bot in Telegram, tap “Start” and choose your interface, learning and translation languages. Premium also offers level selection. You can change your language settings later in the bot.

What is free, and what is included in Premium?

Free access includes a basic selection of words and expressions, basic poems, captioned images, language settings, reminders and a basic personal report. Premium adds A1/A2/B1 level selection for relevant materials, level-based poems, WebApp quizzes and quiz results in the report. Use /premium for current terms. Voluntary support is not payment for Premium and does not grant extra privileges.

How do quizzes and personal progress tracking work?

A Premium quiz has up to 20 questions; the correct answer and example translation appear after your answer has been accepted. In a private chat, /myweek shows only your data for today and the six previous UTC calendar days. With Premium, it includes results and accepted answers from unfinished quizzes. The bot records materials sent, not confirmed reading or mastery. Viewing the report or receiving a reminder does not itself count as a learning day.

Do I need to register separately?

You do not need a separate website account. You need Telegram: open the bot from your account and tap “Start”.
