Short biography

Serhii Pelishenko is a Ukrainian military physician, surgeon and TCCC/CLS instructor who has served since 2021. He plans and coordinates medical care and evacuation, assessing hospital capabilities. He trains military personnel without a medical background: they practise hands-on skills and learn what they can do themselves and when a medic is needed.

He graduated from Zaporizhzhia State Medical University, completed an internship in forensic medicine, and specialised in surgery at Shupyk National Healthcare University of Ukraine. He writes professional articles and develops S-Dose, an offline medicines reference and dose calculator. His working languages are Ukrainian and English.
