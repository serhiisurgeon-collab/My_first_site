My journey

From medicine
to digital solutions

I am a physician by training, a military medic by vocation, and a developer by passion. My journey is shaped by continuous learning, hands-on practice, and a drive to create tools that make complex things simple and accessible.

My professional journey began with medicine — a field where knowledge matters only when it helps you make the right decision at the right moment.

My medical education gave me a strong foundation: an understanding of the human body, clinical reasoning, and responsibility for every decision. My internship and first steps in surgery taught me to work methodically even when there is almost no time for doubt.

Military medicine changed the scale of that responsibility. Here, knowing the protocol is not enough — you must be able to apply it with limited resources, under threat, and under intense pressure.

Teaching became a natural extension of my practical experience. I deliver TCCC and CLS training, create educational materials, and strive to explain complex topics so they can be applied not someday, but immediately after the session.

Over time, technology joined medicine and education. For me, developing apps, bots, and digital tools is not a separate profession for its own sake. It is a way to turn accumulated experience into solutions that can benefit many more people.

I see my path forward at the intersection of these fields: medicine provides purpose, education helps share knowledge, and technology makes it possible to scale practical solutions.
