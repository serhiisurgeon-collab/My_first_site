My professional focus

Medical support, education and digital tools

I am a Ukrainian military physician working in medical planning, the organization of medical support and casualty evacuation. This practical experience shapes how I train military personnel without a medical background and develop tools for medical practice and education.

  1. 01

    Medical planning and support

    Assessing actual capabilities, organizing medical support and planning casualty evacuation.

  2. 02

    TCCC/CLS education

    Preparing military personnel without a medical background to provide care with a clear understanding of their responsibilities and the limits of their training.

  3. 03

    Digital tools

    Developing S-Dose and other digital tools for medical practice and education.
