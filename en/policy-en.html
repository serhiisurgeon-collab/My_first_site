Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what data may be processed while using the website serhiipelishenko.com, why it is needed, and what rights users have.

Last updated: July 22, 2026

01 Who owns the website?

The owner of the website serhiipelishenko.com is Serhii Pelishenko.

For any privacy-related questions, you can contact the website owner by email:

hello@serhiipelishenko.com

02 What data may be collected?

While using the website, the following data may be processed automatically or provided voluntarily:

  • the user's IP address;
  • browser type and version;
  • the device's operating system;
  • the date and time of the website visit;
  • pages viewed by the user;
  • information voluntarily submitted by the user via email.
03 How is this data used?

The data may be used exclusively for:

  • ensuring the stable operation of the website;
  • responding to user enquiries;
  • improving website security;
  • identifying and resolving technical errors;
  • analysing website performance and traffic if relevant analytics services are enabled.
04 Does the website use cookies?

The website may use technical cookies required for its proper and secure operation.

If Google Analytics or other analytics or advertising services are enabled in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Users can restrict or disable cookies in their browser settings.

05 Which third-party services may be used?

Third-party services may be used for hosting, protecting, designing, and operating the website, including:

  • GitHub Pages — for website hosting;
  • Namecheap — for domain name management;
  • Font Awesome — for displaying icons;
  • Google Fonts — for loading fonts, if enabled;
  • Cloudflare — for security and content delivery, if enabled;
  • social networks and external platforms linked from the website.

These services may independently process technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

06 What data is processed when contacting by email?

If a user voluntarily sends an email, the following data may be processed:

  • the user's name or another name provided by the user;
  • email address;
  • message content;
  • files and attachments included with the email;
  • any other information voluntarily provided by the user in their enquiry.

This data is used only to review the enquiry, prepare a response, and continue communication.

07 Is data shared with third parties?

Users' personal data is not sold, exchanged, or shared with third parties, except when:

  • it is necessary for the technical operation of the website;
  • the user has given consent;
  • such disclosure is expressly required by the laws of Ukraine or by a lawful request from an authorised authority.
08 How is data security ensured?

The website uses a secure HTTPS connection and available modern technical measures to protect information.

Reasonable organisational and technical measures are taken to prevent unauthorised access, alteration, loss, or disclosure of data.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information on the Internet can guarantee absolute security.

09 What rights do users have?

Users have the right to:

  • find out whether their personal data is being processed;
  • receive information about the data they have provided;
  • request the correction of inaccurate or outdated data;
  • request the deletion of data where there are no legal grounds for its continued storage;
  • withdraw previously given consent to data processing.

To exercise these rights, users should send a request to the following email address: hello@serhiipelishenko.com .

10 How can I contact you about privacy matters?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or about the processing or deletion of personal data, please contact:

hello@serhiipelishenko.com

11 Article comments

Comments use Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Published comments can be read without signing in. Posting requires a Google account. New comments and replies become public after moderator approval.

Firebase Authentication processes your UID, name, email and Google account information. The site owner has administrative access to this information. Comment documents contain the UID, display name, text, timestamps, article, reply relationship and status. Email, tokens and profile pictures are not added to comment documents.

Published records, including UIDs, names and text, are publicly accessible, including through the API. Hiding the discussion interface does not restrict this access. Pending and hidden records are accessible only to their author and administrators. Do not post patient data or sensitive operational information.

Operational records retain administrator, blocking, latest moderation action and submission-time information. Deleting comment text removes the text and display name but retains the UID, timestamps and reply relationships. It does not delete the Firebase Authentication account or operational records.

Firestore is located in Warsaw, europe-central2. Google/Firebase also processes technical request data, including IP address and browser information, under its own policies. The Firebase SDK retains a local sign-in session in your browser. The comments module adds no advertising or analytics trackers.

The owner creates manual private Firestore backups outside the public repository and website directory. Backups may contain unpublished comments and operational data. Deleted text may remain in older copies; an up-to-date private deletion register is applied before restoration to prevent it from returning to the database. Storage location, encryption, backup frequency and retention periods have not yet been defined. Requests concerning access, erasure or anonymization: hello@serhiipelishenko.com.
